# E-commerce Sales Performance Dashboard — Power BI

## Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing e-commerce business performance across acquisition, conversion, product performance, and profitability.

The project focuses on translating transactional and customer data into business metrics, analytical findings, and actionable recommendations.

## Dashboard Sections

- **Overview** — Overall business performance and key KPIs
- **Acquisition** — Traffic and marketing source performance
- **Conversion** — Conversion funnel and drop-off analysis
- **Product Performance** — Revenue, sales, and product profitability
- **Profitability** — Revenue, refunds, COGS, profit, and ROI
- **Business Glossary** — KPI and metric definitions

## Key Analysis

- Revenue and order performance
- Traffic source and customer acquisition
- Conversion funnel performance
- Product revenue and profitability
- Customer retention and returning sessions
- Refund and profit leakage analysis
